ARRIVAL AND ISLAND TRAVEL

Getting Around

Most visitors arrive by air. Several transportation choices are available.

Rental cars

A local rental agency is located near the airport. Reserve ahead.

Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily.

Taxis and walking

Taxis operate in Taniti City, which is flat and walkable.

Bicycles

Bikes and helmets are available; helmets are required by law.

From the airport by rental car

Pick up a reserved vehicle at the local agency near the airport. Confirm office hours and lodging directions before arrival.

ESSENTIAL VISITOR INFORMATION

Plan Your Trip

Answers to common questions about money, electricity, safety, language, and closures.

What currency can I use?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Major credit cards are widely accepted.

What electrical outlets are used?

Power outlets provide 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Will businesses close for holidays?

Attractions and restaurants may close during national holidays, so confirm hours and plan alternatives.

What about safety and health care?

Violent crime is rare, though petty crime has increased. Taniti has one hospital and several clinics.
